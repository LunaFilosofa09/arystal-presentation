# 3. Quantities taken from sources that were read

This page lists **only** quantities and statements that were taken from a source whose text was read. Where the source is secondary (it quotes data from other authors), that is stated. Quantities that could not be traced to a source read by the project are not listed here.

## Quartz solubility
Equation as given in Takeno et al. (2000), eq. (3), which cites Rimstidt (1997):

    log10 m = −1107.12 / T − 0.0254

with *m* the molality of dissolved silica (mol per kg of water) at equilibrium with quartz and *T* in kelvin. It gives about 11 ppm of SiO₂ at 25 °C, consistent with the 11.0 ± 1.1 ppm that Verma attributes to Rimstidt (1997). Takeno et al. take activities as unity.

## Quartz precipitation and dissolution kinetics
Rate law as given in Takeno et al. (2000), eq. (1): the net rate per unit reactive surface is proportional to (Q/K − 1), where Q is the molality of dissolved silica and K the solubility constant. Equivalent form used here: net precipitation rate = k₊ (S − 1), with S = Q/K.

Rate constant, Takeno et al. (2000), eq. (6), recalculated by those authors from data revised by Rimstidt (1997):

    log10 k₊ = −0.7324 − 3705.12 / T        (k₊ in mol m⁻² s⁻¹, T in kelvin)

Values **computed from this equation** (not read from a table): about 2.6×10⁻¹⁴ at 15 °C, 6.9×10⁻¹⁴ at 25 °C, 2.7×10⁻¹³ at 40 °C and 1.4×10⁻¹² at 60 °C. The source applies it to neutral, dilute solutions and does not consider pH or salinity effects.

## Qualitative statements about iron and colour
- Iron incorporated in the *r* growth sectors exceeds that in the *z* sectors (Cohen & Hassan, 1974, citing X-ray topography by Schlössin & Lang, 1965). No numerical ratio is given.
- Iron is incorporated preferentially along rhombohedral faces, producing hourglass zoning (Troilo et al., 2015).
- Amethyst colour requires iron and also ionizing radiation; iron content alone does not correlate linearly with colour (Cohen & Hassan, 1974).

## Not listed
Quantities that the project could not trace to a source it read (for example face-specific growth rates, iron distribution coefficients, the silica diffusion coefficient in the fluid) are not published here. The current version of the simulator uses provisional values for them (see document 5).
