# 7. High-level roadmap

These are **intentions**, with no commitment to dates; they do not describe current capabilities.

## Near term: from "consistent" to "tested"
- Obtain growth-rate data for the *m*, *r* and *z* faces of hydrothermal quartz, the input that most conditions the simulated habit.
- Compare with simple observations: length-to-width ratio and iron zoning in geode amethysts.
- Investigate the tension between the estimated growth time and geological ages (pH, supersaturation, mechanisms).
- Read the original sources that are currently known only through secondary ones.
- External review of the physical inputs by specialists.

## Medium term
- Distinguish the properties of the *r* and *z* faces, including iron uptake.
- Fluid replenishment and gradual cooling.
- Effect of irradiation and pH on colour and growth rate.
- Extend the regime study to more temperatures and sizes.

## Long term
- Extension to 3D and to other materials.
- A presentation interface for external users.
