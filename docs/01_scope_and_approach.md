# 1. Scope and approach

## Physical system
- **Material:** α-quartz (SiO₂, trigonal system) with traces of iron. Its violet colour comes from iron colour centres activated by natural radiation (Cohen & Hassan, 1974).
- **Environment:** a closed cavity (geode) filled with a low-temperature aqueous fluid carrying dissolved silica and iron. The crystal nucleates on the wall and grows into the fluid.
- **Temperature range explored by the simulator:** 15–60 °C, motivated by fluid-inclusion studies of low-temperature geodes (Arduin Rode et al., 2024; known to the project only at the level of an abstract).
- **Geometry:** a two-dimensional longitudinal section parallel to the c-axis, with the c-axis pointing up. The prism faces (*m*) are vertical; the rhombohedral faces (*r*, *z*) form the inclined termination, about 51.8° from the c-axis (a value the project computed from lattice parameters it has not verified at the source).

## Questions the tool is meant to explore
How do the **shape**, the **iron zoning** and the **growth rate** of a faceted crystal change when temperature, dissolved iron and cooling history change?

## Not in scope for this version
Predicting the growth of a specific amethyst, reproducing measurements, 3D geometry, fluid flow or replenishment, other materials.

## Working principles
- Quantities taken from a source are labelled as verified only if the source text was read.
- Every result separates what is **set by the model's inputs** from what the model **computes**.
- Physical simplifications are documented, and every check states what it does not demonstrate.
